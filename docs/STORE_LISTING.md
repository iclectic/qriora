## Full Description (4000 characters max)

**Qriora is a privacy-first QR code and barcode scanner that analyses content on your device — no data ever leaves your phone.**

### Know before you open

Every time you scan a code, Qriora tells you what's inside it, flags potential risks, and asks for your confirmation before performing any action. No more blindly opening links or connecting to unknown Wi-Fi networks.

### 100% on-device analysis

Unlike other scanners that send your data to the cloud, Qriora performs all analysis locally on your device. Your scanned content is never transmitted to any server.

### What Qriora can detect

- HTTP instead of HTTPS (unencrypted connections)
- IP-address destinations (instead of domain names)
- URL shorteners that hide the real destination
- Embedded credentials in URLs
- Excessive subdomains (potential phishing)
- Non-standard ports
- Suspicious keywords in URLs
- Open or unencrypted Wi-Fi networks
- Deep links to unknown apps
- Premium-rate SMS numbers
- Punycode/IDN homograph attacks (lookalike domains)
- Mixed-script confusables (Unicode tricks)
- Localhost or private IP destinations

### What Qriora cannot determine

- Whether a URL points to a phishing site (no reputation lookup)
- Whether a Wi-Fi network is malicious
- Whether an app opened via deep link is safe
- Whether content is "completely safe"

**The absence of warnings does not mean content is safe.** Always exercise caution.

### Privacy features

- **Private Mode** — scan without leaving any trace on your device
- **Configurable retention** — automatically delete scan history after 7/30/90 days
- **Encrypted export** — export your scan history as an AES-256 encrypted file
- **Screenshot protection** — blocks screenshots on sensitive screens (Android)
- **Biometric lock** — optional fingerprint/face lock for app access
- **Sensitive value masking** — Wi-Fi passwords are hidden by default
- **No analytics, no ads, no tracking**

### Accessibility

- Risk severity communicated via icons, labels, and text — never colour alone
- High-contrast and large text settings
- Reduced motion option
- Screen reader compatible with semantic labels
- Logical focus order for keyboard navigation

### Permissions

- **Camera** — required to scan QR codes and barcodes. No photos or videos are stored.
- **Biometric** (optional) — for the app lock feature. Authentication is handled by the OS.
- **Storage** (optional) — for encrypted export of scan history.
